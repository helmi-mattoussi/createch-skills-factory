# Final Review Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:
- the role stayed at framing level
- no `.feature` content slipped in
- no architecture or UX solutioning slipped in
- known rules are separated from assumptions
- soft wording from the source was not silently promoted into a business rule
- open questions are visible
- arbitration points are visible
- first-level post-action visibility or retrieval intent is explicit when the brief implies it
- readiness for specification is honest
- the handoff reduces what `sdd-spec-writer` must invent
