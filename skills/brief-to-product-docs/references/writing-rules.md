# Writing Rules

- prefer short, operational statements over narrative prose
- keep product language business-readable
- do not collapse uncertainty into confident wording
- treat soft modal language in the source (`probably`, `maybe`, `should`, `would`) as a signal to classify, not as a rule to adopt
- distinguish facts from assumptions explicitly
- keep scope and out-of-scope short and concrete
- state readiness honestly
- end with a handoff that reduces downstream invention
