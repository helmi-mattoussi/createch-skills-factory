# Fixture - Ambiguous Subscription Pause Brief

## Raw input

Some customers ask to pause their subscription instead of cancelling when they are away for a while.

The business wants to keep those customers instead of losing them completely.

Current signals from stakeholders are mixed:
- sales says pause should stop billing so customers feel safe keeping the subscription
- support says pause should mainly stop product access right away
- no one confirmed whether access should remain until the current period ends
- no one confirmed whether billing should stop immediately or only on the next cycle

It should stay simple for the first version.
Do not redesign the full cancellation flow.

Requested behavior hints:
- internal staff should be able to trigger the action
- customers should understand that the subscription is no longer fully active

Not needed now:
- full self-service customer pause flow
- plan switching
- billing proration redesign
- marketing win-back automation

Context:
- subscription management already exists
- customer portal already exists
