# Jira Ticket - Overdue invoice reminder

Need an easier way to remind customers about overdue invoices from the current web app.

Right now people follow up manually and it takes too much time.

Keep the first version simple.

Not needed now:
- full dunning workflow
- custom email editor
- scheduled reminders
- bulk reminder campaigns

Notes:
- the ticket does not define exactly who can trigger the reminder
- the ticket does not define when an invoice becomes reminder-eligible
- the ticket does not define whether repeated reminders need limits
